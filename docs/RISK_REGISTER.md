# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Impact | Current control | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | Runtime behavior is inferred instead of verified. | Incorrect mutation could damage user workloads. | Runtime behavior crosses `RuntimeAdapter`; the narrow mutation gates require live observation, exact identity/ownership, confirmation, and durable intent. Broader lifecycle remains blocked. | Open |
| RISK-002 | Old codename leaks into public-facing docs. | Naming confusion and credibility risk. | `scripts/grep-orchard.sh` scans for accidental references. | Open |
| RISK-003 | Apple container CLI or its user service is unavailable on a target Mac. | Runtime-backed workflows cannot proceed safely. | Doctor performs bounded version/system-status probes through `RuntimeAdapter`, reports `externally-constrained` with remediation, and exits 69 without runtime or state mutation. Provider conformance and distribution-host coverage remain Phase 03/15 gates. | Open |
| RISK-004 | SQLite durability or filesystem handling loses/corrupts local authority. | Lost ownership and recovery evidence could make mutation unsafe. | Schema v13, application ownership, strict database/sidecar identity and permissions, WAL/FULL with full-fsync barriers, serialized Hostwright writers, bounded managed transactions, cancellation/pressure rollback, process-termination recovery, checksummed migrations, online verified backups, atomic restore, projection-only repair, strict maintenance journals, checkpoint recovery, immutable image locks, fenced OCI referrer evidence, and immutable signature/SBOM/vulnerability/provenance evidence are implemented. Same-user direct writers remain unsupported; arbitrary authoritative salvage is forbidden; release-wide hardware-fault and soak qualification remains required. | Open |
| RISK-005 | Networking scope expands into DNS, tunnels, or LAN exposure. | Security exposure and unreliable local behavior. | Current mutation remains loopback-first; Phase 07 owns implemented policy, identity, lifecycle, and cleanup rather than an undocumented expansion. | Open |
| RISK-006 | Brand assets are mistaken for final production assets. | Incorrect public presentation. | Asset README marks PNGs as source material only. | Open |
| RISK-007 | A managed `hostwrightd` could be mistaken for authority over any Hostwright-looking process or launchd record. | Unsafe adoption, termination, replacement, or unattended mutation could affect unmanaged resources. | `hostwright daemon` owns only the exact `dev.hostwright.daemon` plist/status/journal contract, refuses loaded Homebrew or unmanaged/deleted-executable processes, hash-verifies its managed plist, and removes only exact private lifecycle paths after ownership validation. `hostwrightd --service` is still runtime-read-only until the separate unattended-mutation gate passes. | Open |
| RISK-008 | Legacy/default state paths conflict or cross an unsafe filesystem boundary. | Hostwright could select the wrong authority or expose state. | Path precedence is explicit; unsafe chains fail closed; migration requires a compatible ledger, exclusive SQLite lock, same filesystem, identity journal, and unambiguous source/destination. Unknown files are preserved. | Open |
| RISK-009 | Supply-chain metadata is accepted as truth without exact subject, signer, or current-policy verification. | A tampered, stale, or unrelated artifact could authorize unsafe image execution or cleanup. | Gates 6–10 digest-verify exact OCI graphs and subjects; signature, SBOM, vulnerability, and DSSE in-toto/SLSA provenance records bind current policy material and are revalidated for status, lifecycle, recovery, rollback, and exact-cleanup protection. No mutation stdout, tag, filename, state row, or signing secret is authority by itself. | Open |
